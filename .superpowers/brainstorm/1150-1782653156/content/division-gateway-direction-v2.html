BossCrafts Site Direction

A company gateway first, then a division-shaped portal, then one shared site whose tone and guide voice shift by division.

Proposed Entry Flow
Step 1

BossCrafts Gateway

Introduce the company, the philosophy, and the three divisions.

Visitors choose the lens they want to explore through.

Step 2

Division Portal

A short portal page with the selected AI persona, visual flavor, and division framing.

This is the main unique page per division on day one.

Step 3

Shared Company Site

Same core pages and actions, but typography, color accents, and guide voice follow the selected division.

Account signup stores the division choice for public profile and future software sync.

Anvil / Hearthfire
Stoic precision.
Granite, forged metal, warm authority, operational confidence.
Planning Security Infrastructure

Operational and trust-forward

Feels durable, disciplined, and built to last. Best for support, account, governance, and security surfaces.

Technomancy / Runeforge
Chaotic brilliance.
Neon circuitry, volatile intelligence, and hard-edged systems magic.
AI Operating Systems Automation

Experimental but controlled

Feels alive, rebellious, and precise under pressure. Best for AI, OS, research, and tool-building stories.

Ethereal / Halcyon
Luminous imagination.
Cinematic, elegant, expressive, and unapologetically aesthetic.
Creative Editing Worldbuilding

Art-driven and theatrical

Feels emotional, polished, and image-first. Best for portfolio, media, art, and experience-led product storytelling.

Question

Runeforge is now neon green. Should Technomancy feel more like hacker-terminal energy, arcane circuitry, or a balanced mix of both?
