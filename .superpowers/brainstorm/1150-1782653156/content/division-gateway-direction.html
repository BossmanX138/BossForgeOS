BossCrafts Site Direction

A company gateway first, then a division-shaped portal, then one shared site whose tone and guide voice shift by division.

Proposed Entry Flow
Step 1

BossCrafts Gateway

Introduce the company, the philosophy, and the three divisions.

Visitors choose the lens they want to explore through.

Step 2

Division Portal

A short portal page with the selected AI persona, visual flavor, and division framing.

This is the main unique page per division on day one.

Step 3

Shared Company Site

Same core pages and actions, but typography, color accents, and guide voice follow the selected division.

Account signup stores the division choice for public profile and future software sync.
